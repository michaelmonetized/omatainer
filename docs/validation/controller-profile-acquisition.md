# Controller profile acquisition and recovery

The original four-profile [release](https://github.com/michaelmonetized/omatainer/releases/tag/controller-profiles-1.0.0) is published independently of app binaries. The installed optimized Linux ARM64 app downloaded the production HTTPS catalog and all four assets. Their signatures, lengths and SHA-256 values passed, and cached bytes matched the published data exactly. Every asset had owner-only file permissions. The [receipt](controller-profile-acquisition-receipt.json) records binary and source identities, asset hashes and retained test-log hashes.

The native controller panel initially retained bundled instance pins while generation 1 downloaded. **Apply cached profiles** ran while transport and decks were stopped and applied generation 1 to all four connected surfaces. The MPD232's saved local preset stayed separate. The next fresh app build recovered those pins and the cache without another mapping change. Its status reported four open inputs and outputs, two actual introductions, and no feedback failures. APC and MPD inquiry replies are transport evidence; they do not prove physical button responses or lights.

The acquisition owner keeps bundled generation 0 available for explicit rollback after the first authenticated download. A real production-network test exercised download, unchanged active pins, apply, bundled rollback and cache recovery. A separate test ran the real owner in an isolated network namespace: acquisition failed within the request deadline, all four authenticated maps and pin/cache bytes remained unchanged, restart recovered generation 1, and explicit rollback restored generation 0 without lowering the acquisition floor. Neither owner test opened physical MIDI or audio devices.

The installed GUI's current APC control sequence, other-device sweeps, reconnection during performance and physical audio checks remain pending. Earlier observations have their own [powered-hub receipt](powered-hub-controller-qualification.md); they are not automatically assigned to this build. The combined [#511](https://github.com/michaelmonetized/omatainer/issues/511) remains open until its retained acceptance checks pass.
